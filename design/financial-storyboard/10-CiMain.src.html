▾ Why CI compensation matters

Critical illness isn't only a health issue, it's a financial one. Treatment may be covered by the medical card, but the house, car, food, children's school, a caregiver and months without work still need cash.

01Income can stopCancer, heart attack or stroke need long treatment and recovery. Bills and loans don't wait for us to heal.
02Costs outside the medical cardCaregiver, travel to hospital, special food, follow-up care, certain medicines, home modification.
03Family doesn't take over the burdenCash reduces dependence on spouse, children or parents during treatment.
04Focus on recoveryWith a financial runway, treatment and rest aren't forced by "what do I pay tomorrow?".
CI compensation isn't paid when you die. It's there to help you live. Think of CI as a recovery fund and cashflow protection, not just a rider name on the certificate.

How we calculate & assumptions +
  • Monthly need = the higher of (income × share replaced) and fixed commitments.
  • Total need = monthly need × months + extra costs + caregiver's lost income + contributions (if no waiver).
  • Early stage: lump-sum late-only plans pay 0; staged / standalone plans pay the early-stage %. Late stage pays 100%. Booster and recovery allowance are added.
  • Runway = effective sources ÷ monthly need. Suggested cover = early-stage gap, rounded up to the next RM10,000.
