How we calculate & assumptions +
  • Monthly need = income × share replaced (or the itemised total) − other ongoing support.
  • Family support = monthly need × 12, grown each year by the cost-of-living rate, summed over the years chosen.
  • Debts count in full when cover is "not sure" or "not covered"; partly covered debts subtract the amount covered.
  • Have = usable savings + existing benefit for the chosen scenario.
  • Gap = need − have. An estimate for discussion, not a quotation.
