How we calculate & assumptions +
  • Gap = bill scenario − remaining company cover − savings willing to use (minimum 0).
  • Company cover is 0 when self-employed or not working.
  • Future bill = bill × (1 + medical inflation)^5.
  • Years to save = gap ÷ (monthly saving × 12), without returns.
  • Bill amounts are rough upper-end private hospital estimates for discussion, not quotations.
