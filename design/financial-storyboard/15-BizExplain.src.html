▾ Entity type & its effect
Sdn. Bhd. (company)Separate entity. Company assets and accounts don't freeze when the owner dies, but the owner's shares become part of the estate. Keyman can be company-owned; buy-sell resolves the shares.
PLT / LLPSeparate entity (LLP Act 2012). A partner's interest becomes part of the estate; check the LLP agreement for exit and buyout.
Enterprise · PartnershipNot a separate entity. Business debts are the partners' personal debts; a deceased partner's share becomes part of the estate. Buy-sell is critical.
Enterprise · Sole proprietorOwner and business are the same in law. Accounts, stock and assets can freeze until the estate is settled; all business debt is personal. Focus on Enterprise and Loan.
▾ Suitable plan types

Great Eastern Takaful plans only. For Keyman, buy-sell and loans, focus on pure protection (term) from the GE range, named per the current product list. Avoid investment-linked plans for company-owned certificates: the cash value makes tax treatment unclear and adds cost without adding protection.
