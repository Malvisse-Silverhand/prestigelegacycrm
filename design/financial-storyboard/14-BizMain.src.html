If a partner dies, who buys their shares?

Without buy-sell, the heirs inherit the shares. Heirs want cash, the remaining partners want control, and often nobody has the cash. Buy-sell = an agreement + takaful funding, so partners buy at an agreed price and heirs receive cash.

How we calculate & assumptions +
  • Keyman profit method = (net profit + fixed costs × 12) × impact % × months to stabilise ÷ 12 × successor factor + replacement cost. Factors: ready 0.5, second-in-line 0.75, none 1.0. Cash reserve deducted once from the total.
  • Salary multiple = annual salary × multiple. Replacement cost = recruit/train cost + salary overlap during the stabilising months.
  • Keyman CI = impact base × CI months ÷ 12 × factor + 50% of replacement cost − existing CI cover.
  • Buy-sell: share value = business value × holding − existing cover. Cross-purchase: survivors buy in proportion to their own holdings.
  • Loan: guaranteed = debt × % guaranteed; gap = guaranteed − existing loan protection; reserve months = reserve ÷ instalments.
  • Enterprise: cash needed = (instalments + opex + salaries + family income) × transition months; gap = cash needed − existing hibah.
  • Planning estimates only, not a business valuation, quotation, or tax or legal advice.
