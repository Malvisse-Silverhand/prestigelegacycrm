## Compliance rules for BM text

- No guarantee wording about benefits: never "dijamin", "jamin", "jaminan" for benefits, claims or acceptance. The only allowed uses are in a negation ("bukan jaminan", "tidak dijamin") or for a loan guarantor ("penjamin").
- "Could fill the gap" is always "boleh membantu menampung jurang ini, tertakluk kepada terma Helaian Pendedahan Produk (PDS) dan sijil".
- Great Eastern only. No other operator is named.
- "From official illustration" is "Daripada ilustrasi rasmi".
- Numbers and money stay as RM1,234 in both languages.
